What is Blueprintix

Blueprintix is the entrepreneur layer of web3.

You describe what you want in plain English. Blueprintix asks a couple of quick questions, then builds it in front of you: a real, working app you can use, publish and share. No dev team, no code, no waiting weeks for a freelancer.

It's built for web3 first: token sites, dashboards, wallet tools, trackers, community apps, launch pages. But it'll build anything you can describe, web2 included.

You pay in crypto. You sign in with your Solana wallet. That's it.

One line in. Working product out.
02

How it works

1. Describe it

Type what you want, as short or as detailed as you like. “A wallet generator.” “A landing page for my token $FROG on Solana.” “A leaderboard for my Telegram group's best traders.”

2. Answer a couple of questions

If something important isn't clear, Blueprintix asks — usually two or three questions with tap-to-answer options. If your idea is already clear, it skips straight to building.

3. Watch it build

You see the blueprint get drawn, then your app appears in the live preview next to the chat, usually in under a minute.

4. Keep talking to it

“Make the button green.” “Add a dark mode.” “Why does the chart start at zero?” Change anything by chatting, or just ask questions about how it works.

5. Publish it

Hit Publish and your app goes live at its own free address — yourname.blueprintix.fun — and gets listed in the Library, where anyone can open it, read the code, or remix it into their own version. Pick any name of 3–30 letters, numbers and hyphens; names that impersonate wallets or brands are blocked, and a released name is held for you for 7 days. Every live site carries a small “Built with Blueprintix” footer with a report link.

03

What you can build

If it can run in a browser, you can probably blueprint it. Some ideas:

Web3

  • Token landing pages with live price, market cap and holder count
  • Wallet generators and address tools
  • Airdrop checkers and claim pages
  • Portfolio and PnL dashboards
  • Token and NFT trackers
  • Fee and rent calculators
  • Staking and yield calculators
  • Holder leaderboards
  • Mint pages and NFT galleries
  • Community hubs with links, socials and charts

Web2

  • Landing pages and waitlists
  • Calculators and converters
  • Dashboards and internal tools
  • Mini games
  • Link-in-bio pages
  • Portfolios and personal sites

Good to know: right now every build is a single-page app that runs fully in the browser. It can read from Solana and public APIs, but it doesn't have its own server or database yet. Backends are on the roadmap.

04

Web3 projects

Blueprintix only adds web3 features when your project needs them. Ask for a calculator and you get a calculator, not a token page.

When you're building a token or on-chain project (you mention a ticker, a mint or a chain), you get the extras built in:

  • Wallet connect (Phantom)
  • Live on-chain data: price, supply, holders, balances
  • Copy-mint button and explorer links
  • Brand kit: logo, colours and type that carry across the whole page
  • The sections that matter: how to buy, tokenomics, socials, chart

Don't have a mint yet? That's fine. It builds with placeholders you can swap for the real address later by just telling it.

05

Editing and chatting

Everything happens in the chat next to your preview.

  • Change something: describe it. “Move the chart above the stats.” “Make it feel more premium.”
  • Ask something: “How does the price update work?” “Is this safe to use with my real wallet?” It'll answer without changing anything.
  • Polish: after a build, hit ✨ Polish for a second pass that tightens up the design and details. This is optional and uses extra credits.
  • Stop: you can stop a build at any time. You only pay for what was generated.
  • Undo: every version is saved. Jump back to any earlier one.
06

The Library

The Library is where published builds live.

  • Open any project and use it straight away
  • Read the code to see how it works
  • Remix it into your own editor and make it yours

Publishing to the Library is optional. Your build still gets a live link either way. Builds that try to trick or harm people (fake wallet pages, drainers, seed-phrase forms) are blocked and never make it to the Library.

07

Your account

There's no email, no password and no sign-up form.

Try it without anything. Anyone can open Blueprintix and start building in demo mode with 150 free credits. Demo builds aren't saved, so treat it as a test drive.

Connect a wallet to keep your work. Your Solana wallet is your account. Connect it and your projects are saved, your credits live there, and you can publish and remix. Anything you built in demo mode on the same device comes with you.

Blueprintix never asks you to sign a transaction to log in, and never asks for your seed phrase or private key. If anything ever does, it isn't us.

08

Credits

Everything you do in Blueprintix uses credits. Bigger builds use more; small edits and questions use less.

  • Credits are based on real cost. Each action is priced from what it actually costs to run, so a quick question costs a lot less than a full build.
  • You see what you spent. After every build you get roughly what it cost.
  • Stopping a build only charges for what was generated.
  • Failed builds aren't charged.

Rough guide

✨ Polish~30–90

Approximate — credits are metered by the real work done, so exact amounts vary per build.

Your own domain

Every published build is live at a free yourname.blueprintix.fun address. If you want a real domain — yourname.com, .xyz, .io, .app and a few more — you can buy one without leaving Blueprintix.

How it works

  • Open the publish sheet on a live build and hit Get your own domain. Search a name and see what's free, with the price for a year.
  • Pay in crypto, the same way you buy credits. The domain is only registered once the payment is confirmed on-chain.
  • We register it, point it at your build and secure it with HTTPS. You watch each step tick off and get the live link, usually within a few minutes.
  • Your free Blueprint address keeps working. The domain becomes the main link we show.

Who owns it

Domains are registered by Blueprintix on your behalf, with domain privacy on, so you never hand over personal details. It's yours to use: point it at any of your published builds from My domains, and ask for a transfer any time — we unlock it and send you the transfer code.

Renewals

Domains run for a year. We remind you in-app 30 days and 7 days before it ends; renew by paying again. If it isn't renewed, the domain lapses and the build falls back to its free Blueprint address.

If something goes wrong

If a name gets taken between your search and your payment, or a registration fails, you're refunded automatically as credits at the normal rate and told why. Names that impersonate wallets, exchanges or brands can't be bought, same as free addresses.

12

Safety and privacy

  • Your keys stay yours. We never ask for a seed phrase or private key. Apps that generate wallets do it in your browser, and the keys never touch our servers.
  • No harmful builds. Requests for drainers, phishing pages, fake wallet popups or copies of real brands' login pages are blocked.
  • Previews are sandboxed. Builds run in an isolated frame, so a project can't read your wallet connection or anything else on the site.
  • What we log: to run the service and stop abuse, we log your prompts, wallet address, device type and approximate location (from your IP). We don't sell it and we don't track you across other sites.
13

Limits right now

Blueprintix is new and moving fast. Today:

  • Builds are single-page apps that run in the browser (no own server or database yet)
  • Publishing gives you a free yourname.blueprintix.fun address; connecting a domain you own is coming next
  • Very large, complex apps may need to be built in a few steps rather than one prompt

All of these are on the roadmap.

14

FAQ

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe it, it builds it. If you do code, you can read and copy every line.

Do I own what I build?

Yes. It's your project. If you publish it to the Library, others can remix it, so keep it unlisted if you don't want that.

Can I use it for web2 stuff?

Yes. Web3 is our focus, but it'll build anything that runs in a browser.

Which chain does it support?

Solana. Builds read live on-chain data from Solana, and payments are in SOL or USDC on Solana — with $BPIX coming soon.

Is there a token?

Yes — $BPIX is live on Solana. 70% of revenue goes to buying it back, and holders will get discounted and bonus credits. See $BPIX.

Why crypto only?

Because that's how our users already move money. No cards, no banks, no forms.

Something broke. What do I do?

Tell it in the chat what's wrong — that fixes most things. If not, undo to an earlier version, or message us on X: @BlueprintixSol.
